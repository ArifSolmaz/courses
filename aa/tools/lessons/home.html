14 weeks · One connected course

From one record
to a whole network.

Follow the same devices through the course: find a message, organise the records, explore their network and explain why a method fits the task.

In every lesson, trace the steps, explain what the symbols mean, use the result and connect it to the next problem. Start with simple arithmetic, paper and a pencil. No prior Python is required.

The learning route

One question leads to the next.

Lesson languages: Weeks 1–10 have English and Turkish editions. Weeks 11–14 currently open in Turkish; their English editions will be prepared one at a time. Switch language inside each translated lesson.

  1. 01–03 · Read and measure

    Start with incoming messages. Make the result precise, then count and compare the work.

    Week 01Define the resultFind a device’s first message; distinguish its ID from its position.→
  2. Week 02Count the workTurn the trace into n and C(n); count sequential and nested loops.→
  3. Week 03Compare growth, choose a methodCompare 2n with n²; explain when binary search is valid.→

  4. 04–07 · Store, find and order

    Keep the same device records. Choose a structure for the operation you need.

    Week 04Organise the recordsChoose arrays or linked lists; trace FIFO and LIFO operations.→
  5. Week 05Find a device by IDUse a dictionary; trace a search tree, hashing and collisions.→
  6. Week 06Choose the next maintenance jobMaintain a min-heap when inserting a job or removing the minimum.→
  7. Week 07Sort the whole directoryTrace recursive calls and returns, merge sorted lists and explain n log n.→

  8. 08–11 · Explore and connect

    Treat the devices as a network. Distinguish reachability, dependencies and costs.

    Week 08Send a message through the networkBuild adjacency lists; use a FIFO queue and BFS to find hop distances.→
  9. Week 09Respect maintenance dependenciesTrace DFS and its call stack; detect a cycle or build a valid topological order.→
  10. Week 10Connect all devices at minimum costUse Kruskal; track components and reject edges that create a cycle.→
  11. Week 11Find cheapest paths from one sourceUse Dijkstra and relaxation; explain the nonnegative-weight condition.→

  12. 12–14 · Constrain, reuse and verify

    Add an exact edge count, store repeated answers, then ask what a certificate proves.

    Week 12Use exactly k edgesEnumerate walks with a choice tree, backtrack and prune safely.→
  13. Week 13Reuse answers to repeated subproblemsBuild the k = 4 DP table in order; reconstruct a walk from predecessors.→
  14. Week 14Check a proposed solutionSeparate finding from verification; reason about certificates, P/NP and reductions.→
