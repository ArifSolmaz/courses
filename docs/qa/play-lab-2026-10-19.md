# Play Lab visual and interaction audit — 19 October 2026

Local preview only. No commit or push was performed for this audit.

## Scope and method

Browser UI walkthroughs and saved screenshots at 1366×768 (laptop) and 390×844 (mobile layout). Additional 320×740 narrow-layout checks covered the four shared Adventure worlds and Factory. These are browser viewport checks, not physical-device or cross-browser certification.

| Game | Classic coverage | Adventure coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beat the Robot | All 3 stage layouts, 14 m winning route and 16 m tie | All 4 stages: initial and successful result, both sizes; over-budget failure |
| Command Centre | All 3 stage layouts; wrong job, FIFO completion, stack undo | All 4 stages: initial and successful result, both sizes; wall collision, intermediate movement, new map |
| Depot Detective | All 3 stage layouts; empty-slot deletion failure, tombstone success; 7-node skewed tree and 7-step search; capacity feedback | All 4 stages: initial and successful result, both sizes; deletion failure; alternate non-colliding data |
| Sorting Arena | Both algorithms; mixed, nearly sorted, reverse layouts; 3- and 8-part custom data; values 1 and 20, duplicates and all-equal data; invalid input, wrong choice, completion | All 4 stages: initial and successful result, both sizes; over-budget failure; second contract including the two short value-1 bars from the reported defect |
| Factory Rush | All 3 modes at both sizes; six-delivery completion, late deliveries, repair success and wrong undo | All 6 stages: initial and successful result, both sizes; losing plan and retry, new day, intermediate delivery animation, deliberate waiting and weighted objectives |

Animations were exercised through normal UI actions. Sorting/depot Adventure currently reveal the computed result rather than animate every individual comparison/probe; this audit does not claim those detailed animations exist. Long mobile pages scroll normally; the whole page is not compressed into one phone screen.

Screenshots were reviewed individually and in contact sheets. Some full-page captures produced a transient compressed rendering; the affected desktop depot/sorting initial screens were rechecked with ordinary viewport captures (`verified-desktop-*`). The capture artifact was not treated as a website layout defect.

## Corrections

- Shared Sorting Adventure: explicit number/face line heights and minimum bar height keep faces inside the smallest bars.
- Classic Sorting: more minimum visual height and bottom padding for low-valued parts, including 1 among 20s.
- Factory Adventure: picked/disabled job cards keep readable text rather than fading the information needed to compare plans.
- Factory timeline: start–finish text moved beside the bar. Bars now retain exact proportional widths without a minimum width that distorted short jobs or clipped labels.
- Narrow Factory layouts: job cards wrap into readable rows rather than squeeze five cards into one row.
- Classic Command Centre: initial feedback now describes the current stage (jobs, undo, or route building). Depot sandbox feedback now prompts storing/finding a key.
- Versioned changed assets so the preview and subsequent publication load the matching CSS/JS together.
- Previously prepared short, stage-specific Turkish Adventure instructions remain included in the local changes.

## Automated checks

All seven test files passed:

- Core: 7,920 rail maps, hashing/deletion/wrap/full-table/reference-set checks, BST and undo.
- Sorting: 1,080 arrays with duplicates, both algorithms, wrong-choice invariance, sorted/reverse counts for sizes 3–8.
- Adventure: four worlds, 48 route/grid/sort cases, all available sorting candidate plans, exhaustive depot plans, queue/stack paths and deletion trap.
- Classic Factory: FIFO/LIFO, 320 free-choice shifts, invalid actions, wait/deadline counts and snapshot undo.
- Leaderboard model: replay validation, score ordering, wait tie-break, shared ranks, best-only updates, safe names and stored data.
- Factory campaign: fixed level targets and 100 generated days checked against an independent scheduling oracle.
- Workshop model: 15 automatic evidence checks; final reflection remains self-reviewed.

## Boundaries

No live leaderboard score was submitted. The shared backend was not redeployed. The Workshop exercise pages are not new Adventure games and were not included in this visual walkthrough (their existing model checks passed). Generated games have many possible inputs: representative UI scenarios plus exhaustive/parameterized model tests were used, not a claim that every possible play sequence was visually inspected.
