Physics I

Thirteen weeks of notes. Each week opens with what to do before class, the worked examples in full, and the figures you can read off.

Start with week 01 Course dashboard

The weeks

  • Week 01Measurement, units, physical quantities and vectorsUnits, dimensions, physical quantities and vector components
  • Week 02Motion in one dimensionPosition, velocity, acceleration and free fall
  • Week 03Motion in two dimensions and Newton’s lawsProjectile components, Newton’s three laws and a first free-body diagram; friction applications follow next week
  • Week 04Friction and applications of Newton’s lawsForce diagrams, static/kinetic friction, inclines and connected bodies
  • Week 05Work and kinetic energyWork, kinetic energy, net-work theorem and power; potential/conservation energy begins in Week 08
  • Week 06Problem solving and reviewReview only the units, motion, forces and work/kinetic-energy material already taught; 29 October holiday week
  • Week 07MidtermNo new topic; use the review notebook and the announced examination arrangements
  • Week 08Potential energy and conservation of energyGravitational/spring potential, conservative forces and energy accounting, including dissipative work
  • Week 09Linear momentum, impulse and collisionsImpulse, momentum conservation and elastic/inelastic collisions
  • Week 10Rotation of rigid bodiesAngular kinematics, radian conversion and moment of inertia; torque-driven dynamics follows in Week 11
  • Week 11Dynamics of rotational motionTorque, angular acceleration, rotational energy and rolling
  • Week 12Equilibrium and centre of massCentre of mass, force balance, torque balance and choosing a pivot
  • Week 13Periodic motionSpring-mass motion, pendulum period, energy and initial conditions; damping as an optional extension
