# PHY101 — Textbook, OCW and laboratory map

**Why this exists.** The weekly notebooks are self-contained, but a second explanation in another voice is
often what makes a topic land. This table gives, for every week, the matching chapter in the four standard
calculus-based introductory texts, a free online equivalent, and the departmental experiment.

**Türkçe:** Haftalık notlar kendi kendine yeterlidir; ama bir konuyu asıl oturtan şey, çoğu zaman aynı
konunun başka bir sesle ikinci kez anlatılmasıdır. Bu tablo her hafta için dört standart kitaptaki
karşılık bölümü, ücretsiz bir çevrimiçi kaynağı ve bölüm deneyini verir.

**If you buy nothing, use OpenStax.** [University Physics Volume 1](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-1) is free, legally
downloadable and complete for this course. The paid texts are listed because many students already own
one, and because the common exams are written at their level.

**Kitap almayacaksan OpenStax kullan.** Ücretsiz, yasal olarak indirilebilir ve bu ders için yeterlidir.

## What is deliberately *not* in this course

| Topic | Standard chapter | Why not here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circular motion, in its own right | Y&F 3.4, Serway 6, OpenStax 6.4 | Covered where it is needed, plus the optional `extensions/Circular_Motion.ipynb` |
| Angular momentum | Y&F 10.5–10.6, Serway 11 | Introduced inside Week 11 and extended in `extensions/Angular_Momentum.ipynb` |
| **Gravitation and Kepler's laws** | Y&F 13, Serway 13, HRW 13, OpenStax 13 | Not on the departmental topic list, so **not examinable**. Offered as `extensions/Gravitation_and_Orbits.ipynb` because it is the single biggest gap against every standard text. |
| Fluids | Y&F 12, OpenStax 14 | Not on the departmental topic list for PHY101 |
| Waves and sound | Y&F 15–16, OpenStax 16–17 | Beyond this course; optional `extensions/Waves_and_Sound.ipynb` |
| Thermodynamics | Y&F 17–20 | PHY102 |

**Examinable scope.** The common midterm and final are written against the departmental topic list for
Weeks 01–13 only. Extension notebooks, supplements and laboratory analysis technique are enrichment and
are **not** examined.

**Sınav kapsamı:** Ortak vize ve final yalnızca 01–13. haftaların bölüm konu listesine göre hazırlanır.
Ek notlar ve laboratuvar analiz teknikleri sınav kapsamı dışındadır.

## Laboratory strand

Eight sessions from the departmental *Deney* column, with the shared
[measurement and uncertainty toolkit](labs/Lab_00_Uncertainty_Toolkit.ipynb).

The experiments deliberately run **behind** the lectures: the Week 04 experiment measures Week 02
physics, and the Week 05 experiment measures Week 03 physics. The one exception is the simple pendulum in
the Week 11 session, whose lecture treatment is Week 13 — [Lab 11](labs/Lab_11_Inertia_Pendulum.ipynb) §3
therefore contains a self-contained primer, and Week 11's notebook carries a short preview.

**TR:** Deneyler derslerin gerisinden gelir; bu kasıtlıdır. Tek istisna 11. hafta oturumundaki basit
sarkaçtır: ders anlatımı 13. haftadadır, bu yüzden Lab 11 §3 kendi kendine yeterli bir hazırlık içerir.
